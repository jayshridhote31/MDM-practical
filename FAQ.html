Student Department Help Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about your courses, campus facilities, or exams? Explore our guide below.

Applications open within 14 days of exam score publication. You can submit your request directly via the Student Academic Portal under Examinations > Re-evaluation. Please ensure receipt payment is uploaded before submission.
A minimum of 75% attendance is compulsory in both lectures and practical labs. For cases between 65%–74% due to medical conditions, submit an official doctor's certificate countersigned by your Faculty Advisor.
Graduate researchers and final-year capstone students can apply for evening lab passes. Submit the Lab Safety & After-Hours Form approved by your supervising professor at the Department Office.
All lecture slides, syllabus copies, and the 5-year archives of past examination papers are available on the Department Digital Library. Login with your student ID credentials.
Contact your mentor or subject professor at least 3 weeks before your application deadlines. Prepare your CV, current transcript, and list of target institutions in your email inquiry.
